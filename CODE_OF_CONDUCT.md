# Code of Conduct

The `dfsc` project is committed to a respectful, inclusive, and technically
constructive community.

Participants should discuss ideas and evidence rather than personal
characteristics, provide reproducible information when reporting numerical
issues, respect data and software licenses, and avoid harassment or
discriminatory conduct.

Project maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues, or contributions that
violate these expectations. Serious or private concerns may be reported to
Haitao Duan at `duanhaitao@hdu.edu.cn`.
