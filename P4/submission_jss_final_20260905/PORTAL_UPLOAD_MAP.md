# P4 Editorial Manager upload map

1. Manuscript: `manuscript/dfsc_primitive_protocol_en.pdf`
2. Editable manuscript source: `manuscript/dfsc_primitive_protocol_en.tex`
3. Bibliography and style files: `manuscript/references.bib`,
   `manuscript/elsarticle-harv.bst`, and `manuscript/placeins.sty`
4. Figure files: all PDFs under `manuscript/figures/`
5. Supplementary material: both files under `supplement/`
6. Highlights: `editorial/01_highlights.txt`
7. Title page: `editorial/02_title_page.txt`
8. Cover letter: `editorial/03_cover_letter.txt`
9. Declaration of competing interest:
   `editorial/declaration_of_competing_interest.docx`
10. Author biographies: `editorial/author_biographies.docx`
11. Data statement: `editorial/05_data_and_code_statement.txt`
12. Graphical abstract, if selected: `graphical_abstract/graphical_abstract.pdf`

The evidence matrix and text declaration are internal cross-checking aids and
need not be uploaded unless the editor requests them.
