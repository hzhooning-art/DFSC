# P5 Editorial Manager upload map

1. Manuscript: `manuscript/manuscript_en.pdf`
2. Editable manuscript source: `manuscript/manuscript_en.tex`
3. Bibliography: `manuscript/references.bib`
4. Figure files: all seven PDFs under `figures/`
5. Highlights: `editorial/01_highlights.txt`
6. Title page: `editorial/02_title_page.txt`
7. Cover letter: `editorial/03_cover_letter.txt`
8. Declaration of competing interest:
   `editorial/declaration_of_competing_interest.docx`
9. Data statement: `editorial/05_data_and_code_statement.txt`
10. Graphical abstract, if selected: `graphical_abstract/graphical_abstract.pdf`

The evidence matrix and text declaration are internal cross-checking aids and
need not be uploaded unless the editor requests them.
