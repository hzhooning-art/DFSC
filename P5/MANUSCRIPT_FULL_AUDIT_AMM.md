# P5 AMM Manuscript Full Audit

Audit date: 2026-08-29

## Editorial conclusion

The manuscript now has one primary line of argument rather than a collection of software, fitting, and application results:

> Sparse grouped relaxation experiments can reward additional exponential modes without resolving additional timescales. Adjacent-rate sensitivity coalescence explains this gap. A higher order is therefore promoted only when information gain, held-unit transfer, rate stability, and local separation agree; otherwise the scientifically valid output is unresolved.

This line closes the sequence from practical problem, mathematical obstruction, decision method, controlled validation, intended material use, and falsification-oriented scope tests.

## Research gap and response

- **Practical pain:** extra fitted poles are frequently interpreted as physical relaxation times even under finite windows, correlated residuals, and non-independent channels.
- **Research gap:** reconstruction and prediction methods do not by themselves specify when a shared dynamical order is experimentally resolved.
- **Mechanism:** adjacent exponential sensitivity columns coalesce as rates approach each other, making local inversion unstable.
- **Response:** the manuscript defines a grouped four-criterion support rule and an explicit unresolved state.
- **Theory:** a two-point testing bound and projected-sensitivity rank condition provide local, design-conditional justification.
- **Evidence:** known-truth controls validate decisions; PVA and copper-alloy data test intended material use; gas and hydraulic records test conservative refusal; four controlled extensions audit assumptions.

## Remaining limitations that should remain visible

1. The theoretical guarantees are local and design conditional, not distribution free.
2. PVA has only three independent specimens; its bootstrap interval is descriptive.
3. The main validated model uses grouped observations on a declared common grid and positive real finite decay rates.
4. Public external tasks do not supply microscopic ground-truth mechanism labels.
5. Independent external reproduction and a frozen archival software DOI remain pending.

These limitations constrain interpretation but do not break the central argument. Removing them from the manuscript would make the claims less credible.
